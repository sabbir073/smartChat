Why this page exists

A chat widget has to work on ordinary pages: long ones, short ones, pages with sticky headers, pages the visitor arrives on from somewhere else. This site provides those pages so the widget is tested against something that behaves like the web rather than a blank document.

Northwind Supply — a fictional company used to test the SmartChat widget end to end.
